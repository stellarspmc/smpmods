# AudioPlayer Usage
> There are 3 crucial commands: 
> `/audioplayer apply` -> to apply your music
> `/audioplayer url` -> to add the music into the game
> `/audiplayer range <range>` -> to control the range of the music played

## YouTube Audio Tutorial
1. Get a URL from a YouTube video by pressing the "Share" button.
2. Put the URL into [the converter](https://ezconv.cc/en/youtube-to-mp3) **or any other converters you would like**.
3. Paste the YouTube URL you got into the bar, then click convert. 
4. Press download MP3 File.
5. Place the MP3 file into [the uploader](https://pages.edgeone.ai/drop)
6. Press `Select File`, then find your MP3 file. 
7. Press `Start Deployment`.
8. Press `Visit Site` and copy the URL.
9. Now in Minecraft, type `/audioplayer url` and copy your URL **with quotation marks** 
   * (example: `/audioplayer url "https://mc.spmc.fun"`). 
10. It will give out `Success!` and press `Put ID on item`. 
11. You need to hold a **Player Head, Music Disk or Goat Horn** to work. 
12. then type `/audioplayer apply {}`, where {} is your ID. 
13. Then use your goat horn, put a music disc in the jukebox or put the player head on a note block to play the music.

- You can use `/audioplayer range <range>` to set the range of its radius.
- To keep the music in the player head after placement, break the player head with **silk touch**.
